About Me

I am a theoretical and computational researcher working on the dynamics of complex systems, with a focus on neural systems. I combine analytical approaches with large-scale simulations to uncover principles of emergence, information processing, and collective behavior.

Neural Dynamics

Understanding how neural networks generate collective activity, oscillations, and temporal patterns.

Complex Systems

Studying how collective phenomena emerge from interactions among many simple components.

Theoretical Modeling

Building minimal yet biologically grounded models to reveal mechanisms underlying complex behavior.

Computational Methods

Developing efficient simulations and computational tools for large-scale complex systems.

Research

My research explores how microscopic interactions, network structure, and dynamical rules give rise to collective phenomena in complex systems.

Neural Network Dynamics

Investigating emergent activity, excitation–inhibition balance, oscillations, memory, and information processing in recurrent neural networks.

Complex Networks

Studying how network topology, modularity, connectivity, and interactions influence collective dynamics.

Transient Synaptic Memory

Developing minimal biologically grounded models in which finite synaptic activity creates a transient memory of recent network states.
