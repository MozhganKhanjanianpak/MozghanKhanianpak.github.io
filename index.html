Mozhgan Khanjanianpak, PhD

Complex Systems Physicist & Computational Neuroscientist

I am a complex systems physicist working at the intersection of complex networks, dynamical systems, and theoretical neuroscience.

My research explores how interactions among neural elements and network structure give rise to collective dynamics, oscillations, memory, and information processing in biological neural systems.

My research journey began with complex networks and epidemic spreading and has gradually moved toward understanding the dynamics and computational principles of biological neural networks.
